# Executive Copy Guard Protocol

## 1. Positioning Mandate
Dawn Maley is a Senior C-Suite Executive Operations Partner and Fractional Online Business Manager (OBM)—never position her as a generic entry-level "Virtual Assistant."

## 2. Vocabulary Rules
- **USE Concrete Operational Terms:** "Calendar Defense," "Inbox Triage Architecture," "Executive Bandwidth Recovery," "Cross-Functional Studio Logistics," "Gatekeeping & Stakeholder Routing," "Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)."
- **BAN Weak/Generic Phrases:** Never use "helping busy people," "passion for organization," "wearing many hats," "admin helper," or "miscellaneous tasks."

## 3. Case Study Structure Rule
Every work history item must strictly render in three scannable parts:
1. **The Brief** (The operational bottleneck or executive stakes)
2. **The System Built** (The concrete protocol or workflow engineered)
3. **The Outcome** (Quantifiable hours saved, friction removed, or velocity gained)
