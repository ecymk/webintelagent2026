## 1. Design System & Aesthetic Direction
- **Vibe:** "Quiet Luxury Meets Studio Command" — editorial authority combined with tech-founder precision.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background Primary: `#0A0A0A` (`bg-neutral-950`)
  - Surface / Card: `#141414` (`bg-neutral-900/70`)
  - Border Subtle: `#262626` (`border-neutral-800`)
  - Text Primary: `#F5F5F5` (`text-neutral-100`)
  - Text Muted: `#A3A3A3` (`text-neutral-400`)
  - Accent Gold (Executive Authority): `#FBBF24` (`amber-400`)
  - Accent Emerald (ROI / Recovery Metric): `#34D399` (`emerald-400`)
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (tight tracking `-0.02em`, bold/semibold)
  - Data / Metrics / Badges: `JetBrains Mono` or monospace utility class (`font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-xs`)
